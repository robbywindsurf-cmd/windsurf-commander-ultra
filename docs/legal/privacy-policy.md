# Privacy Policy

**Windsurf Commander** — last updated 30 September 2026

## Who we are

Rob Wilson, trading as **Windsurf Commander** ("we", "us", "our"), operates the
Windsurf Commander mobile application (the "App"). We are the **data
controller** for the personal data described in this policy, which means we
decide why and how it is used.

- Contact for anything in this policy, including data requests: **robbywindsurf@icloud.com**

If you are in the UK, your data is protected by the UK GDPR and the Data
Protection Act 2018. If you are in the EEA, the EU GDPR applies. Your local
supervisory authority is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) in the
UK, or your national authority in the EEA.

## The short version

- **Your videos and photos never leave your device.** They are processed on
  your phone and are never uploaded to us.
- **Pose analysis and AI coaching run entirely on your device.** The coaching
  model is a file on your phone, and the prompts built from your data are not
  sent to any AI provider.
- **We show no ads, we do no cross-app or cross-site tracking, and we never
  sell your data.**
- The personal data that does reach our servers is your **session summaries**
  and **banded body measurements**, so your history can be backed up across
  devices and used for anonymised peer comparison.

## What stays on your device

None of the following is uploaded to us:

- Videos and photos you import or record
- The results of pose and technique analysis (joint angles, force estimates)
- The AI coaching model, and the prompts built from your own data
- Your local database of sessions, equipment, notes, weather and tide records

If you use the App's backup feature, an export of that database is written to
a file **you** choose. What happens to that file is under your control.

## What we collect, and why

### Account and sign-in

- **Sign in with Apple.** We receive an identifier for your account, your
  email address (which may be Apple's private relay address), and your name.
  Apple only gives us your name on the **first** sign-in; we store it, and
  Apple never sends it again. We do not receive your Apple ID password.
- **Nickname and password.** If you sign in this way, your password is hashed
  **on your device** using SHA-256 and only the resulting key is sent to us.
  We never receive or store your actual password. Because the same nickname
  and password always produce the same key, this is what lets you reach the
  same account from another device.

### Session and activity data

When you import a session file (FIT, CSV or GPX) it is read on your device.
If you are signed in, the following summary is sent to our servers so your
history syncs across devices:

- Session date, start and end times, duration
- **GPS coordinates** for the session, and a count of track points
- Peak and average speed, distance
- **Average and maximum heart rate**, and calories
- Board, sail and fin used
- Sport type, and any session name or notes you entered

### Body measurements (optional)

If you fill in the Rider Profile, you can give your height, weight, inside leg
length and arm span. These are used on your device to calculate forces and to
derive a **banded category** (for example a height of 170–179 cm becomes a
band, not an exact figure).

When you analyse a video, we send that **category and its bands** — never your
exact height, weight, leg length or arm span — together with the biomechanics
results for the analysed clip (knee angles, foot pressure percentages, fin
load, lever factors) and the conditions (wind speed, wave height, board
volume, fin and sail size). We use this to build **anonymised aggregate
figures** so riders can compare themselves against similar riders. These
aggregates are statistics across many riders; they are not published
individually and are not linked back to you by name.

### Weight log

If you log your weight over time, those readings stay on your device and are
used to show you your own trend.

### Subscriptions and purchases

Purchases are handled by **Apple** and by **RevenueCat**, our subscription
management provider. We receive whether you have an active subscription and
which tier it is. We do not receive your card details, and no payment
information passes through our servers. See Apple's and RevenueCat's own
privacy policies for how they handle that data.

### Weather and tide

Weather is fetched from **Open-Meteo** using the coordinates of the beach or
session concerned. Tide data comes either from readings you import yourself or
from a public source. Open-Meteo receives the coordinates of the request; it
does not receive your account details.

## Who we share data with

We share personal data only with the providers that make the App work:

| Provider | What they receive | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Oracle Cloud** (our hosting) | The session summaries and banded measurements described above | To store and sync your data |
| **Apple** | Purchase and subscription events | To process payments |
| **RevenueCat** | An app user identifier and subscription status | To manage entitlements across devices |
| **Open-Meteo** | Coordinates for the location requested | To return weather forecasts |

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers or
data brokers. We may disclose data if we are legally required to, or to
establish or defend legal claims.

## What we never do

- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
- No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs that profile you
- No tracking you across other apps or websites
- No selling or renting your personal data
- No sending your data to an AI provider — coaching runs on your device

## Our legal bases for processing

Under the UK GDPR we rely on:

- **Contract** — to create and run your account, sync your sessions, and
  provide the subscription you have paid for
- **Consent** — for optional information you choose to give, such as body
  measurements, and for the anonymised peer-comparison upload. You can
  withdraw consent at any time by not providing it, or by contacting us
- **Legitimate interests** — to keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and
  improve how it works, balanced against your rights

## Heart rate data

Heart rate readings come from files you import, such as a watch export. Heart
rate information can reveal things about your health, which makes it
**special category data** with extra protection under the UK GDPR. We process
it on the basis of your **explicit consent**, given when you choose to import
a file that contains it. If you would rather not share it, you can sign out
before importing, or contact us to have it removed. We use it only to show
your own session history and statistics — never for medical purposes, and
never to make health assessments.

## How long we keep it

- Account and session data: while your account is active, then for as long as
  we need it for legal or accounting purposes
- Subscription records: as long as tax and accounting rules require
- Anonymised aggregate statistics: indefinitely, because they no longer
  identify anyone
- Local data on your device: until you delete it, uninstall the App, or
  restore a different backup

## Where your data is stored

Our servers and database are hosted with Oracle Cloud in the **UK/EEA**, so
your account and session data stays within the UK/EEA.

Some of the third-party providers in the table above are international
companies that may process limited data outside the UK/EEA — for example Apple
and RevenueCat for purchases, and Open-Meteo for weather requests. Those
transfers are handled under those providers' own safeguards. We do not
otherwise transfer your personal data outside the UK/EEA.

## Security

Your data is sent over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed on your device and never
transmitted. Your local data sits inside the App's protected storage on your
phone. No system is perfectly secure, but we take these steps to protect
yours.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- **Be informed** about how we use your data (this policy)
- **Access** a copy of your personal data
- **Correct** data that is wrong or incomplete
- **Delete** your data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Restrict** or **object to** certain processing
- **Port** your data — move it to another service in a usable format
- **Withdraw consent** where we rely on it

To exercise any of these, email **robbywindsurf@icloud.com**. We will respond within
one month. If you are unhappy with how we have handled it, you can complain to
the ICO (ico.org.uk) or your local supervisory authority.

The App also lets you export your full local database as a backup file, which
you can keep or use to move your data.

## Children

The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
their personal data. If you believe a child has provided us with personal
data, contact us and we will delete it.
